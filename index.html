About our school

A warm first school for confident young learners

Temple of Education is a kindergarten school dedicated to helping every child feel welcomed, respected, and excited to learn. Our classrooms balance phonics, numbers, storytelling, art, music, movement, manners, and guided play so children grow academically, socially, and emotionally.

We proudly praise the school community—teachers, parents, helpers, and students—for creating an environment where kindness, discipline, creativity, and curiosity are practiced every day.

Programs

Age-appropriate learning paths

Playgroup

Gentle separation, sensory play, rhymes, motor-skill activities, and friendly routines.

Nursery

Language readiness, number concepts, storytelling, coloring, craft, music, and outdoor play.

LKG & UKG

Early reading, writing, mathematics, environmental awareness, life skills, and school readiness.

Why parents appreciate us

Effort that shows in every smile

  • Individual attention that helps children settle quickly and learn at their own pace.
  • Dedicated teachers who celebrate small achievements and build confidence.
  • Regular activities, celebrations, and parent updates that make school life memorable.
  • Clean, colorful spaces designed for safety, comfort, creativity, and joyful discovery.

SC definition

School Culture means safety, care, and confidence

At Temple of Education, SC stands for School Culture: the daily habits, values, and routines that help children feel safe, cared for, and ready to participate.

Our school culture is built through respectful greetings, tidy classrooms, guided sharing, patient listening, joyful activities, and consistent encouragement from teachers and parents.
